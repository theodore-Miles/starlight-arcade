Block Raiders

Roam space. Shoot ships apart. Steal their blocks. Build a bigger ship.

Every ship is made of blocks. Hit the yellow core and the whole ship falls apart — every block still in one piece floats free. Fly into floating blocks to grab them, then press E to open the ship builder.

Ships from three gangs fly all over space — Crimson, Amber and Lilac. They fight each other, so you can grab the leftovers. They leave you alone unless you fly too close or shoot them — then they hunt you down, and so do their friends nearby. A yellow ? means a ship is getting suspicious — back off! A red ! means it's hunting you. The farther you fly from home, the tougher the ships and the better the parts.

Gang ships grab floating parts too, so they get bigger over time. The bigger your ship gets, the more gangs see you as a threat: they spot you from farther away and come after you faster. Watch your Threat level! Shields only protect the parts close to them, so spread them out. Put a Radar next to your guns to turn them into turrets, then click (or tap) any part of an enemy ship to lock on — your turrets will shoot at exactly that part. Add a Scanner to get a mini map that shows ships around you. The bigger your ship, the bigger your build area.

In the builder you can add parts, sell spare parts for scrap ⚙, and spend scrap to upgrade blocks. Upgraded blocks are tougher, and upgraded weapons hit harder and fire faster.
